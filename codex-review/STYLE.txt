STYLE:
An artist's travel sketchbook page: a squared notebook whose faint blue grid shows through, drawn with crayon-bright posca paint markers and a black fineliner. The DRAWINGS are quick, loose, freehand — a few confident gestures that suggest rather than describe, caught in seconds: posca lays the masses first as broad flat chalky strokes of colour, then the fineliner adds just enough ink to sharpen an edge, a silhouette, the detail that makes the thing recognisable. No hatching, no architectural rendering, no laboured shading — colour and gesture do the evoking. Palette: electric blue, sunny yellow, warm coral, leaf green, violet — cheerful, in confident doses, white paper still winning. The LETTERING is the opposite of the drawings: neat, careful, evenly spaced — the looseness belongs to the pictures only. Airy page, wide gutters of bare paper, drawings kept small, nothing crowded. Hand-made and alive. NOT vector art, NOT clipart, NOT a 3D render, NOT a stock icon, no gradients, no drop shadows, no glossy highlights, no digital perfection. The characters are blank oval-headed silhouettes. Never draw any logo or brand name — draw the shape the logo evokes instead.
